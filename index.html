ABOUT ME

I'm a computer science graduate from the University of Cambridge, currently working as a graduate software engineer on the GPU team at ARM.

I am interested in working with graphics and low level programming, along with just about everything else.

I have developed multiple projects with Vulkan, including a Voxel raymarcher for my dissertation project. I have also designed, and had manufactured, multiple PCBs for CUSF (Cambridge University Spaceflight) and for myself, along with writing the code for them with C, including writing the interfaces for the sensors.
